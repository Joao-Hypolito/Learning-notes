# 📌 English Learning Journal: Active Writing Templates

**Date:** Oct 22, 2026  
**Focus:** Moving from passive reading to active writing using structural templates (Technical / Architectural English).

---

## 💡 Key Takeaway
Instead of memorizing isolated words, focus on mastering **sentence structures (carcaças)**. Words come with daily exposure and practice, but knowing how the structure functions is what builds active writing confidence.

---

## 🛠️ Sentence Templates & Practice

### 1. Class / Entity Function
* **Structure:** `The [Entity] implements [Interface] and serves as [Function].`
* **Purpose:** Describes the main responsibility of a component in the indicative mood.
* **Example:** 
  > *"The workshop uses a sander and a jigsaw and serves as a workspace for my diorama."*

### 2. Problem & Solution
* **Structure:** `To eliminate [Problem A] and handle [Problem B], [Subject] uses [Solution].`
* **Purpose:** Explains technical goals and how tools or patterns address them.
* **Example:** 
  > *"To eliminate burrs and handle asymmetry, the workshop uses sandpaper."*

### 3. Cause, Effect & Value
* **Structure:** `Through [Mechanism], [Subject] remains [State], preserving/saving [Value] and preventing [Risk].`
* **Purpose:** Highlights the architectural benefits or long-term outcomes of a choice.
* **Example:** 
  > *"Through hard work, the diorama remains complete, saving money and preventing time waste."*

---

## 📝 Grammar & Concept Notes
* **Mood:** These structures use the **Simple Present (Indicative)**, not the Imperative. They describe *how things work* rather than giving direct commands.
* **Vocabulary Nuance:** 
  * Use `saving` instead of `preserving` when referring to time or money (*saving money*).
  * `Sandpaper` is uncountable (no article needed before it).
  * `Burrs` (rebarbas) is countable and usually used in the plural.
